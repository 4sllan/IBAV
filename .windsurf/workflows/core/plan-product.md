# /plan-product

## Purpose

Define the high-level planning before creating a detailed spec.

## Steps

1. Review the output of `/analyze-product`.
2. Clarify the problem or opportunity to address.
3. Define the goal, scope, and non-goals at a high level.
4. List constraints and dependencies.
5. Identify risks and open questions.
6. Output a planning document or summary.

## Output

- Goal
- Scope
- Non-goals
- Constraints
- Risks
- Open questions
- Recommended next step: `/create-spec <feature-name>`

## Constraints

- Do not implement code.
- Do not create detailed specs unless requested.
- Stop and wait for approval before proceeding to `/create-spec`.
